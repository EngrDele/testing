# SITE LIFE: A Game That Replaces the Safety Induction Quiz

*Concept note: the "Lagos Life" game idea applied to workplace safety induction*

---

## 1. Your story: the problem we are solving

You arrive at a company as a new worker, contractor or visitor. They say: *"We need to do your induction first."*

The induction is supposed to show you **how the site is set up: where the hazards are, the rules, the emergency exits and the muster point.** In practice it goes like this:

1. You sit in a room and **watch a video on the TV.**
2. They give you a **paper with questions.** You tick the answers.
3. They mark it. If you pass, you go in.

It has been done this way for years, and it has these weaknesses:

| Weakness | What happens in real life |
|---|---|
| Watching is passive | People drift off, sleep in the AC, or press their phones. |
| The paper tests memory, not judgement | Ticking "B" on paper is not the same as **noticing** a blocked fire exit while you are busy. |
| Answers get shared | It is the same paper every time, so answers go around the gate and the canteen. |
| The video is generic | It is often foreign footage. It does not show **this** compound. |
| It happens once | People forget most of it within weeks. |
| A tick proves attendance, not competence | This is weak evidence after an accident. |

**The idea: turn the induction into a game.** Instead of paper questions, the person **plays through real-life scenarios of this same compound** before they enter the real one.

---

## 2. The inspiration: Lagos Life

**Lagos Life** is the viral Nigerian browser game launched on **1 October 2026**.

| Fact | Detail (as reported) |
|---|---|
| Creator | **Shalom Rayhamen**, a Nigerian developer. The company is **Vatar, Inc.** |
| What it is | A free, **browser-based, Sims-style life simulator** set in Lagos. It is multiplayer and runs on phone and desktop with **nothing to download.** |
| International original | **The Sims** (Maxis/EA, 2000), the classic life simulator. Lagos Life took that idea and made it fully Lagos. |
| How you play | A **"birth lottery"** decides your start, from rich "Nepo Baby" to "LAPO Baby" in debt. You look after **6 needs**. You apply for jobs on an **in-game phone** (about 25–33 careers with levels), pay **weekly rent**, and move around by **trek, danfo, keke, okada, cab or car**. The map includes Lekki, Yaba, Mushin, Surulere and landmarks such as CcHub, Freedom Park and Afrika Shrine. NEPA can take light. |
| Growth | About **1M players in 3 days**, about **5M by 9 Oct** and about **7M in 10 days**. These are the company's own figures. |
| Money | Free to play. Revenue comes from **virtual billboards**, plots, music promotion and corporate partnerships, with more than **$170k** reportedly earned or contracted. |
| Funding | **$500k pre-seed at a $10M valuation** about 8 days after launch. Investors include Resilience17 (Flutterwave CEO), Terra Industries' Nathan Nwachuku and Mono's Abdulhamid Hassan. This is self-reported and unaudited. |

**Why it went viral:** people saw **their own reality** in it, including danfo, NEPA, the landlord and real places. It is **free, instant and runs in the browser**, and it is **social** because everybody plays in one city.

**What SITE LIFE takes from it:**
1. **A familiar style of game set in a place you know.** Lagos Life uses Lagos. SITE LIFE uses **this company's own compound.**
2. **Runs in the browser with no download.** The visitor scans a QR code at the gate and plays on their own phone.
3. **Needs bars:** Lagos Life has 6 needs. SITE LIFE has **6 "Body" bars**. Unsafe choices damage them.
4. **Birth lottery becomes Role lottery.** You may be given the role of visitor, contractor, forklift driver, cleaner or electrician, and each role meets different hazards.
5. **In-game phone:** tasks, permits, the emergency number and reporting a near-miss.
6. **Real local life:** NEPA takes light and the generator comes on, rain floods the yard, an okada drops you at the gate, and "Oga" tells you to "just do it".
7. **Billboards:** inside the game, the billboards carry the **company's own safety messages**. Later they can be **sponsored by PPE suppliers or insurers**, which is how Lagos Life makes money.

> We are copying the **idea and style**, not Lagos Life's code, name or artwork. SITE LIFE needs its own name and brand.

## 3. How SITE LIFE works

### 3.1 The player's journey (≈ 20–30 minutes)

```
 SCAN QR AT GATE / RECEPTION
        │
        ▼
 1. ROLE LOTTERY ─ "You are: CONTRACTOR – Electrician. Your task today: fix a light in the Warehouse."
        │
        ▼
 2. SHORT INTRO (2–3 min clips inside the game, filmed in THIS compound) – replaces the long TV video
        │
        ▼
 3. YOUR FIRST SHIFT – walk the compound map (real photos / 2D map of the site)
      • Each zone = a scenario. You make choices. Consequences show immediately.
      • Hidden hazards to spot and tap ("Spot it, Report it").
      • Random events: NEPA takes light, rain, fire alarm drill, "Oga" pressure.
        │
        ▼
 4. EMERGENCY! – the fire alarm sounds. Find the nearest exit and get to the MUSTER POINT in time.
        │
        ▼
 5. END-OF-SHIFT REPORT – Body bars, hazards found, mistakes + "why", PASS / REPLAY
        │
        ▼
 6. DIGITAL BADGE on phone + record sent to HSE → security lets you in
```

### 3.2 The 6 Body bars (in place of Lagos Life's 6 needs)

| Bar | What damages it |
|---|---|
| ❤️ **Health** | Cuts, falls, being struck by a forklift, burns |
| 🫁 **Lungs** | Generator fumes (CO), chemical vapour, dust without a mask |
| 👂 **Hearing** | Entering a noisy area without ear protection |
| 👁️ **Eyes** | Grinding or chemical splash without goggles |
| ⚡ **Life** | **Life-critical mistakes** such as electrocution, a fall from height or entering a confined space. One wrong choice can end the shift: "You go home in an ambulance. Replay." |
| 🛡️ **Site Safety** | Shared by **everyone on site**. Unsafe acts and unreported hazards reduce it for all players. |

### 3.3 Example compound map

Each company gets its own map, built from **its own photos and layout**. Example:

```
┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│  MAIN GATE   │  RECEPTION   │  ADMIN       │  CANTEEN     │
│  (security,  │  (sign-in,   │  (stairs,    │  (gas, hot   │
│   okada)     │   PPE issue) │   cables)    │   oil)       │
├──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│  CAR PARK    │  FORKLIFT    │  WAREHOUSE   │  WORKSHOP    │
│  (reversing  │  ROUTE       │  (racking,   │  (grinding,  │
│   vehicles)  │  (walkways)  │   ladders)   │   noise)     │
├──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│  GENERATOR   │  ELECTRICAL  │  CHEMICAL    │  DIESEL TANK │
│  HOUSE (CO)  │  ROOM (LOTO) │  STORE (SDS) │  FARM (fire) │
├──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┼──────────────┤
│          LOADING BAY (trucks, chocks)       │ 🟢 MUSTER    │
│                                             │    POINT     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────┘
```

### 3.4 Example scenarios (written in English or Pidgin, with Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo versions)

**Main gate:** An okada drops you. Your hands are full and you have no ID. Security says: "Go in, I know you."
- Go in → ❌ *The muster count won't include you in an emergency.* Site Safety −1
- **Wait and sign in for a visitor pass** → ✅

**Generator house:** NEPA has taken light. The generator is running, the door is closed because of the rain, and a colleague is sleeping inside.
- Leave him → ❌ Lungs −3 and Site Safety −2. *Generator fumes contain carbon monoxide. You cannot smell it, and it kills people who are sleeping.*
- **Open the door, wake him, bring him out to fresh air and report** → ✅ +3
- *Na quiet killer be gen smoke.*

**Forklift route:** You want to cut across the forklift lane to get to the canteen faster.
- Cut across → ❌ Health −4. *The driver cannot see you behind the load.*
- **Use the yellow walkway** → ✅

**Warehouse ("Oga" event):** Your supervisor says: "Abeg climb that rack, bring the box. No time for ladder."
- Climb → ❌ ⚡ **Shift over: you fell from height.**
- **Say "I no fit do am like that". Use the Stop-Work right and get a ladder or the order picker** → ✅ +5 and the 🏅 *"Stop-Work Hero"* badge

**Electrical room (electrician role):** You need to change a breaker.
- Start work, "e no go take time" → ❌ ⚡ Electrocuted
- **Get a permit, isolate the supply, lock and tag it out (LOTO), test it is dead, then work** → ✅

**Random event, rainy season:** Water is entering near the electrical panel.
- **Keep away, warn others and call the electrician or the emergency number on your in-game phone** → ✅

**Hidden hazards to spot:** These are placed randomly every time, so there are no answers to share. Examples: a fire extinguisher behind cartons, a frayed extension cable, a missing machine guard, an unlabelled chemical bottle, a truck with no wheel chock and a blocked fire exit.

**Emergency at the end:** The alarm rings. The player must use the **correct exit route** and reach the **muster point** within the time limit. Using the lift or going back for a bag fails.

### 3.5 Pass rules: competence, not luck

- **Pass:** you finish the shift with no ⚡ life-critical failure, at least 80% of hazards found, and you reach the muster point in time.
- **Fail:** you see each mistake with a short **"why"**. You replay, and the scenarios are reshuffled.
- Questions and hazards are **randomised from a large bank**, so sharing answers does not help.
- Every run is logged: **name, role, score, mistakes, time and date.** HSE gets a **dashboard**, which gives a much stronger audit trail than a ticked paper.

### 3.6 Multiplayer (like Lagos Life's shared city)

- A group of new staff can play the **same compound together**. When one person reports a hazard, everyone sees it fixed. When one person ignores it, everyone's Site Safety bar drops.
- This teaches: **"Your unsafe act fit wound me too, so I go talk."**
- There is a monthly **league between departments**.

## 4. Where it fits in the induction

| Old way | SITE LIFE way |
|---|---|
| 20–40 min TV video | 2–3 min clips **inside the game**, filmed in the real compound |
| Paper quiz, tick answers | **Play your first shift** in the virtual compound (20–30 min) |
| Same paper every time | **Randomised** scenarios and hazards |
| Pass mark on paper | **Pass on competence**: no life-critical errors, hazards found, reached the muster point |
| Paper filed in a cabinet | **Digital record** and HSE dashboard |
| Forgotten after day 1 | **Weekly 1-minute scenario** on WhatsApp or the app. The best real near-miss each month becomes a new scenario in the game. |

**Optional extra for workers who will be on site long term** (not needed for visitors):
- A short **real hazard hunt** in the actual compound, with planted hazards to tag.
- A **practical fire extinguisher** drill.

**Versions:**

| Who | Version | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor or delivery driver | **Gate Edition**: QR at the gate, green scenarios, muster route | 5–10 min |
| Contractor | Role-based shift with permit-to-work scenarios | 20–30 min |
| New staff | Full shift and multiplayer session | 30–45 min |
| Supervisors | **"Oga Mode"**: you are the supervisor, the pressure is on you, and you decide | 30 min |

## 5. Business angle (the same way Lagos Life makes money)

- **Each company pays for its own edition.** We photograph the compound, build its map, write its scenarios and set up its HSE dashboard. After that there is a **monthly subscription** per site.
- **In-game billboards** carry sponsored adverts from PPE suppliers, insurers or safety training schools, the same way Lagos Life sells billboards.
- **Sector packs** cover manufacturing, oil and gas, construction, warehousing and logistics, hospitals and schools.
- **Partners** could include the Lagos State Safety Commission ("Safety First" and "Working Safely"), IOSH training providers and insurers.
- **Standards:** it supports **ISO 45001** competence (7.2), awareness (7.3) and worker participation (5.4). Confirm the exact legal duties with your HSE or legal adviser.

---

## 6. Proving it works (pilot)

1. Choose one site. Give one intake the **old way** (video and paper) and the next intake **SITE LIFE**.
2. At **day 1, day 30 and day 90**, test both groups with the same set of **photo scenarios** and a hazard-spotting task.
3. Also track **near-miss reports per person** (more reports is good), first-aid cases and feedback ("Would you speak up to Oga?").
4. Decide after 3 months.

Research on serious games for safety is positive but still small. One controlled study found that game-based construction safety training beat traditional training and that the knowledge lasted. That is why the pilot measures results.
