## 2026-10-15: Warehouse Shift (clean dashboard look)
- Trigger: the owner sent a video of a logistics dashboard ("see how clean this is, the new episode of our safety game should be clean like this and make it about warehouse"). We copied the *style* only (light isometric 3D, royal-blue warehouses, white cards, KPI tiles, inspect panel, stepper, tabbed live list), not the product's name, logo or brand.
- Guidance followed: UK HSE *HSG76 Warehousing and storage* (vehicle/pedestrian segregation, reversing and banksmen, trailer creep and keys control, rack damage and the SEMA green/amber/red scale, load notices, battery charging: hydrogen, ventilation, PPE, eye-wash), ILO Occupational Safety and Health Convention (C155) principles, ISO 45001 (hazard reporting, emergency preparedness), Nigerian Factories Act (PPE, fire exits kept clear, first aid), forklift pre-use checks (tag out a faulty truck).
- Built `warehouse.html` (`src/warehouse-game.js` + `src/warehouse-shell.html`): 8-step shift (gate & PPE → receiving dock → forklift check → put-away & racking → picking → battery charging → loading & dispatch → emergency), 12 questions with a "why" for every option, the FL-03 pre-use checklist in the inspect panel, 6 hidden hazards + 3 safe decoys found through the inspect panel, live forklifts that stop for people, trucks that arrive, reverse, dock and leave, near-miss counting, NEPA blackout, fire alarm and muster. Click-to-move with A* (walkways preferred, forklift aisles and reversing zones avoided), WASD, drag/pinch/scroll camera with eased fly-to. Phone layout: the cards collapse into a bottom sheet. Full Pidgin. Results post as `game: 'warehouse'`; SCORM package `site-life-warehouse.zip`; group sessions and trainer events work on our server.
- Tests: `node tests/playthrough.js warehouse` (every stop, one deliberate wrong answer, all hazards, pass screen, posted result, screenshots `tests/out/warehouse-*.png`), also with `GAME_LANG=pcm` (no untranslated strings); room, offline and SCORM tests cover the new page; `factory`, `little` and `home` still pass.
- Next: show it to the owner on a real phone; try it with a real warehouse client (their racking layout and dock count could become a site file like the factory's); recorded Pidgin voice-over for the questions.

## 2026-10-13: Little Stars (children 3–6)
- Guidance followed: WHO *World report on child injury prevention* (burns, drowning, poisoning, falls, road traffic: supervision, safe storage, covering water containers), WHO hand hygiene (soap and water, about 20 seconds), Red Cross / fire-service "get low and go out", "stop, drop and roll", NSPCC "PANTS" / Underwear Rule (private parts, no body secrets, say no, tell someone), common road-safety advice that children under 7 cannot judge traffic.
- Built `little.html` (`src/little-game.js` + `src/little-shell.html`): a sticker-book menu, 10 dollhouse scenes, tap only, everything spoken (`SL.speak`, captions on) with picture cards, no losing (gentle retry, hint after 2 misses), a sticker per scene saved on the device, a one-line tip for the parent, "Play 5 in a row". The parent's phone number for the sing-along stays in localStorage and is never sent. Full Pidgin. Results post as `game: 'little'`, `result: 'COMPLETE'`; SCORM package `site-life-little.zip`.
- **Review needed:** the body-safety scene ("My body is mine") must be reviewed by a child-protection professional before it is used in schools. The scene, its parent tip card and the code say so.
- Tests: `node tests/playthrough.js little` (all 10 scenes, a deliberate wrong tap, both posted results, screenshots in `tests/out/little-*.png`), also with `GAME_LANG=pcm`; `node tests/playthrough.js home` still passes.
- Next: try it with a nursery class and parents (do 3-year-olds manage the cards?), record real voice-overs in English and Pidgin instead of the device's speech synthesis, and get the body-safety review done.
