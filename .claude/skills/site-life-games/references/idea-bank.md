# Idea bank by age

Starting points only. Check `safety-game/research/IDEAS.md` for what is already built or rejected, and add a source for the real danger when you pick one.

## Age 4 (Little Stars scenes)
**Built:** hot, water, medicine, socket, road, fire, lost, body, hands, seat.

| Idea | The danger | How it plays |
|---|---|---|
| Gas smell | LPG leaks are common in homes | Sniff, then don't touch the switch or the matches, then tap Mummy |
| Stairs and windows | Falls are a leading child injury (WHO) | Tap the open window or the stair gate; "hold the rail" |
| Dogs | Dog bites, rabies | Stand still like a tree; don't touch a dog that is eating |
| Choking | Small objects, groundnuts | Sort "too small for mouth" items; "sit down to eat" |
| Sun and drinking water | Heat | Hat, water, shade cards |
| Mosquito net | Malaria | Tuck the net in at bedtime; spray is for big people |
| Generator smoke | Carbon monoxide | "Generator smoke is bad; stay away; tell a big person" |
| Sharp things | Knives, broken glass | Tap to "leave it, call a big person" |

## Age 8 (kids worlds)
**Built:** home, school, market, water, online.

| Idea | The danger | How it plays |
|---|---|---|
| Flood day | Seasonal Lagos flooding (NEMA) | Don't walk in moving water; NEPA cables in water; tell an adult; school closed |
| Kitchen helper | Burns, cuts, gas | Help cook with Mummy: pot handles in, knife rules, gas smell |
| Party and dogs | Dog bites, crowds | A birthday party in a compound: dogs, balloons, small toys, electricity |
| Farm or village visit | Snakes, wells, animals, fire | A visit to Grandma's village |
| Sports day | Heat, injuries, water | Warm up, drink water, report injuries |
| Church/mosque crowd | Crowds, getting lost | Hold hands; meeting point; find a helper |
| Rainy-season roads | Gutters, potholes, okada | The walk home in the rain |
| First aid helper | Burns, nosebleeds, cuts | Cool water on a burn, pinch the nose, tell an adult |

## Age 12 (challenge mode or new pages)
**Built:** Online Safety (8–14), Warehouse Shift (adult challenge).

| Idea | The danger | How it plays |
|---|---|---|
| Home alone (with a little sibling) | Gas, generator CO, door, cooking, sibling safety | Afternoon at home: keep the 4-year-old safe, answer the door safely, generator outside only, gas off |
| First aid hero | Burns, bleeding, choking, calling 112 | Scenarios in sequence; give a clear address on the call; recovery position |
| Walk to school alone | Crossing, okada and keke, strangers, being seen | Route planning, crossing without an adult, saying no to okada |
| Flood and canal | Drowning in canals and floods | Peer pressure to swim in a canal; flood evacuation plan |
| Fire at home | Gas cylinder, extinguisher, escape plan | Escape plan, cylinder leak, which extinguisher, smoke |
| Online 2: social media | Scams, sextortion, privacy, footprint | Settings, a fake giveaway, "send pics" (tell an adult, don't pay), a group-chat dare |
| Kites and power lines, NEPA cables | Electrocution | Kite season; fallen cables after rain; "wet hands and switches" |
| Cooking basics | Burns, gas, knife | Make indomie safely: pot handle, gas, the cylinder valve |
| Road at night | Being seen | Bright clothes, the torch, facing traffic where there is no walkway |
