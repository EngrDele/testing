# Suggesting games to the owner

The owner reads on a phone and dictates by voice, so keep suggestions short, plain and practical. They care most about their three children (4, 8, 12), real Nigerian situations, a realistic or clean look, and not changing what already works.

## Format for a reply
Give 3–5 ideas, best first:

> **1. Home Alone (age 12).** Afternoon at home with a little brother: gas smell, generator, someone at the gate, cooking indomie. *Why:* LPG and generator fumes injure children every year (source). *Plays like:* walk the house, keep your brother safe, timer and score.

Then say which one you recommend and offer: "Shall I build it?" Don't build without a go-ahead unless the owner already asked you to build, or the weekly routine is running.

## Scoring (the same table as `research/IDEAS.md`)
Rate 1–5 for each, and add a score:
- **Real harm:** a source shows it hurts children or workers in Nigeria.
- **Fit for the age:** right for 4, 8 or 12 (see `ages.md`).
- **Learnable by playing:** a clear decision the child can practise.
- **Buildable now:** fits an existing recipe in one session.
- **Fun:** movement, surprise, a "what would you do?" moment.

Prefer covering all three ages over time. If the last built game was for age 8, lean towards 4 or 12 next.
